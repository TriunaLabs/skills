What changes when the light moves?

Look at an object near a window. What happens to its shadow as the light changes?

  1. Notice the shadow’s shape and direction.
  2. Predict what would change if the light moved.
  3. Move the light safely and explain what you observe.

Reflection

Say what changed

Ask the learner which observation surprised them and what they would test next. A good answer can begin with “I noticed…”
