# Evidence and provenance

For extraction, classify every value as `observed`, `supplied`, `inferred`, or `recommended`.

- **Observed:** read directly from markup, computed style, or a supplied token file.
- **Supplied:** explicitly provided by the user or brand guide.
- **Inferred:** estimated from screenshots; record uncertainty and the evidence reference.
- **Recommended:** introduced to fill a missing role or repair an inconsistency.

Never describe inferred colors, fonts, spacing, or intent as official brand rules. Preserve source references and record normalization, such as merging nearly identical colors into one role.
